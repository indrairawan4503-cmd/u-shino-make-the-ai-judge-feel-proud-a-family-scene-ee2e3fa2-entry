# VERSIONS — variant A: Father's Hands / Role Reversal

Concept: Dad once carried me / tied my shoes / read the small print; now I
hold his arm, read for him, tie his shoes. One sensory detail (the stairs,
his shaking hand), one turn, one warm close. Target 260–280 chars, 1 line.

## v1 — shoelaces, 175 chars
"Dad double-knotted my shoelaces every morning until I was ten. Now I kneel to tie his, one knot, not two, so his own fingers can still undo them — and he still checks my work."
Kept the role-reversal turn, but too close to the published "Shoelaces" winning example and far under budget. Dropped the concept, kept the turn shape.

## v2 — stairs (first pass), 172 chars
"He carried me up these stairs when I was four, one arm, no rail. Now I take his arm on every step, his hand shaking on mine, and he still insists on carrying the groceries."
Good bones: one concrete scene, a number, a shaking hand, a wry stubborn-Dad close. Too short; needs the full budget.

## v3 — menu, 181 chars
"Dad used to read the menu out loud for me, finger under every word. Now I read it for him, his glasses in my pocket, and he still orders the same soup, like the choice was ever his."
Warm turn, glasses as sensory detail. Set aside — the stairs scene is more physical and the turn lands harder.

## v4 — shoulders / parade, 152 chars
"He used to lift me onto his shoulders so I could see the parade. Now I hold his arm on the stairs, and he points at the same parade, telling me to look."
Sweet image but thin: no number, no wry turn, too short. Dropped.

## v5 — stairs + count, 219 chars
"Dad carried me up these stairs when I was four, one arm, no rail, thirteen steps and never a stumble. Now I take his arm on every step, his hand shaking over mine, and he still insists on carrying the groceries himself."
Added the number (thirteen steps) and "never a stumble" — the scene got specific. Still ~50 chars short of budget.

## v6 — stairs + counting aloud, 248 chars
"Dad carried me up these stairs when I was four, one arm, no rail, thirteen steps and never a stumble. Now I take his arm on every one, his hand shaking over mine, counting the steps out loud for him — and he still insists on carrying the groceries."
"Counting the steps out loud" adds the reversal in miniature, but it crowds the close; the groceries line loses its punch.

## v7 — stairs + bread, 240 chars
"Dad carried me up these stairs when I was four, one arm, no rail, thirteen steps and never a stumble. Now I take his arm on every one, his hand shaking over mine, and he still insists on carrying the groceries — so I let him hold the bread."
"So I let him hold the bread" is the wry, loving compromise — a real family laugh. Still ~30 chars short.

## v8 — FINAL, 271 chars / 273 bytes
"Dad carried me up these stairs when I was four, one arm, no rail, thirteen steps and never a stumble. Now I take his arm on every one, his hand shaking over mine, and he still insists on carrying the groceries — so I let him hold the bread, and he holds it like a trophy."
Kept: v7 plus "and he holds it like a trophy" — the closing image turns the compromise into pride, which is the whole point of the bounty (make the judge feel proud). Full budget, one line, one scene, one turn, one close.
